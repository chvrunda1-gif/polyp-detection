Post-Polypectomy & Colonoscopy Care Instructions

Clinical guidelines based on the US Multi-Society Task Force on Colorectal Cancer.

Recovery (First 48–72 Hours)

Avoid heavy lifting, intense cardiovascular workouts, and non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) unless approved by your doctor. If you experience fever, acute abdominal pain, or significant rectal bleeding, contact your clinic immediately.

Dietary & Lifestyle Health

Maintain high dietary fiber intake (25–35 grams daily) with fresh fruits, leafy vegetables, oats, and legumes. Drink at least 2 liters of water daily and moderate intake of processed red meats.
