Under The Hood

Endoscopic AI Inference Pipeline

From wet mucosal frame acquisition to pixel-level lesion boundary localization.

STEP 01
Preprocessing & CLAHE
Inpaints harsh specular reflections and applies Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization on the LAB color space to enhance mucosal vascular pit patterns.
STEP 02
CNN Classification
Deep binary convolutional neural network evaluates global feature maps, producing probability of polyp presence and confidence score.
STEP 03
U-Net Segmentation
Four-level encoder-decoder with skip connections synthesizes low-level spatial texture and high-level semantics to segment exact lesion borders.
STEP 04
HUD & Clinical Metrics
Extracts lesion contour, computes bounding box reticle, estimates Paris classification, calculates millimeter diameter, and formats clinical export.

How Polyp Detection Operates in Practice

1
Patient Intake & Image Submission

Patients upload their high-definition endoscopy frames securely. Images are queued directly for attending physician evaluation.

2
Dual-Engine Deep Inference (CNN + U-Net)

The attending doctor launches the AI Detection Centre to run binary polyp detection, Paris morphology estimation, and pixel-level semantic mask segmentation.

3
Comparative Split Inspection & Doctor Sign-Off

Physicians compare raw mucosal tissue against HUD segmentation overlays, verify or override AI findings, and append clinical impressions.

4
Official Diagnostic Report Dispatch

Upon doctor sign-off, a cryptographically hashed clinical report is archived. Patients receive clear, reassuring next steps in their patient portal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clinical & Technical Details

What dataset is used to train and validate PolyScope? +
PolyScope is designed for datasets such as Kvasir-SEG and Hyper-Kvasir, which provide thousands of expert-annotated polyp images and verified segmentation masks from routine colonoscopies.
What happens if pre-trained deep learning weights are missing? +
PolyScope includes an integrated, deterministic Computer Vision Feature Extractor that computes color-opponency and morphological contours out-of-the-box. The active engine is always transparently labeled.
How does the system ensure data security and HIPAA alignment? +
All API requests are protected with CSRF tokens, passwords are encrypted using salted scrypt hashing, and role-based access control segregates patient records from unauthorized viewers.
